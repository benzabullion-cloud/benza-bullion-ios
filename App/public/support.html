Common questions

How do I update my account?

Open Settings to change your Display Name or email and update your password. Use the Notifications tab to manage notification preferences.

I forgot my password. What should I do?

On the login screen, choose Forgot password?, enter your Benza Bullion email address, and use the recovery link sent to your inbox to set a new password.

Can I download a copy of my data?

Yes. Open Settings and choose Export My Data. Benza Bullion creates a personal JSON export containing your account metadata, holdings, activity, portfolio history, price alerts, and notification preferences.

How do I delete my account?

Open Settings and choose Delete Account. The app requires confirmation before permanently deleting your Benza Bullion account and associated portfolio data. Deleting your account does not cancel Apple subscriptions. Billing continues until you cancel through Apple subscription settings; you can still delete your account immediately.

Why can market prices differ from a dealer quote?

Benza Bullion displays third-party reference market prices. Physical bullion products may trade above or below those prices because of premiums, spreads, taxes, product availability, and dealer pricing.

Are portfolio values financial advice?

No. Benza Bullion is a tracking and informational tool. Market data, portfolio calculations, alerts, and educational content are not investment, tax, or legal advice.
